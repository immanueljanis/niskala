# Product

## Register

brand

(The landing page is the brand surface and the primary judging surface. `/trade` and `/rfq/[id]` are product surfaces and follow the product register.)

## Users

- Hackathon judges and crypto-native builders arriving from the Monad Metropolis submission: they give a page 30 seconds to answer "what is new here" and want to verify it on chain.
- Professional traders and liquidity takers: want firm, competitive execution and evidence the router did not play favourites.
- Market makers: want to compete without putting losing prices on a public book.

## Product Purpose

Niskala is a sealed RFQ market on Monad. Three makers commit hidden quotes; a zero-knowledge proof verified by a contract shows the winner is the best valid quote across the complete committed set; only the winning price is revealed; settlement is atomic. Success: a visitor understands the loop (request, seal, prove, settle) in one scroll, trusts that every step is real (transactions, proof, verifier), and opens the terminal to run a round.

## Brand Personality

Sealed, exact, nocturnal. The voice of a private execution desk at 2 a.m.: calm, specific, slightly cryptographic. Confidence comes from showing real artefacts (commitments, proof hashes, gas, transactions), not from adjectives.

## Anti-references

- The generic AI landing page: centered hero, three identical icon cards, eyebrow labels above every section, no imagery, no motion, buzzword copy.
- DeFi-casino and neon cyberpunk: gradient blobs, particles everywhere, 3D coins, giant APY numbers, bouncy animations.
- Glassmorphism-by-default and purple/blue gradient text.
- "Private Uniswap" or dark-pool framing; never claim strategy privacy is novel or that the prover is blind.

## Design Principles

1. Show the artefact, not the adjective: real hashes, real transactions, real timings carry the trust.
2. One idea per fold: request, seal, prove, settle each get their own moment.
3. Motion is state: things seal, lines draw, numbers resolve; nothing loops for decoration.
4. Privacy is drawn, not described: sealed stays visibly sealed next to the one revealed price.
5. Honest edges: the coordinator boundary is on the page, not in a footnote.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on dark surfaces (body text ≥ 4.5:1). Every animation has a reduced-motion fallback (instant or crossfade). Keyboard-reachable controls; status conveyed by text, not colour alone.
